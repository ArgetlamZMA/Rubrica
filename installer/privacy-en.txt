RÚBRICA PRIVACY

Rúbrica works with your documents on this computer. It has no accounts, collects no usage statistics and does not send your documents to servers of Rúbrica or ZMA Inversiones.

Connections the application makes:

- GitHub: at startup it checks whether a new version exists (it sends only the Rúbrica version). You can turn this off in Settings > "Check for updates at startup". Updates are only installed if their signature is valid.
- OneDrive and Google Drive: only if you connect your account and open or save there.
- Timestamp server (for example, the Honduran SAR's TSA): only when signing with a timestamp; a cryptographic digest of the signature is sent, never the document.

OCR, conversion, digital signing and signature verification run locally.

Full policy: https://github.com/ArgetlamZMA/Rubrica/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
